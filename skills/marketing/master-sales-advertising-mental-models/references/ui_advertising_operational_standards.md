# UI & Advertising Operational Standards for qnt. Platform

## Critical User Preferences & Design Execution Rules

1. **Competitor-Free Sovereign Hooks:** Never name or compare against competitors in social media or marketing hooks. Establish qnt. as the category-creating standard and benchmark.
2. **Luxury Cream & High-Contrast Canvas Architecture:**
   - On `#FAF8F5` canvas: Use pitch-black/deep-navy ink typography (`#0B0F1D`, `#060811`, `#1E293B`).
   - Never allow white or light-cyan text to sit directly on light/cream backgrounds without a dark card wrapper.
   - On dark mode (`#060811`), explicitly enforce dark CSS styles to prevent mobile browser whiteouts.
3. **High-Contrast Interactive Toggles & Filter Tabs:**
   - Active pill: Solid pitch-black background (`#0B0F1D`) with pure white bold text (`#FFFFFF`) and dark shadow (`0 4px 12px rgba(11, 15, 29, 0.25)`).
   - Inactive pill: White background with crisp border and slate-700 text (`#334155`) that highlights on hover. Never dark-grey on dark-grey.
4. **Real 3D Currency Assets & Pure Branding:**
   - **No Redundant Logo Artifacts:** Never add redundant letters/badges next to the main brand (e.g. do not put an isolated "Q" box beside "qnt."). Keep the brand mark pure: `qnt.` with an electric cyan accent dot.
   - **Real 3D Currency Assets:** When crafting financial hero sections, embed physical 3D multi-currency assets ($ USD, £ GBP, ₹ INR, € EUR) with perspective, metallic gradients, bevels, and floating animation.
5. **No Cluttered "Terminal Live" Banners in Header:** Keep the brand header clean and institutional: `"qnt. SOVEREIGN WEALTH OS | 200 Quantitative Vehicles Verified"`.
