# 3D Liquid Prototyping & Multi-Variant Architectural Design System

## Overview
Operational framework for engineering multi-prototype suites of bespoke 3D WebGL web applications with non-identical geometries, distinct pastel color schemes, non-repeating typography, and specialized financial calculation tools.

## 1. The 5 Rules of High-Fidelity 3D Multi-Prototyping
1. **Zero Geometry Cloning**: Every prototype must run a completely distinct Three.js WebGL scene:
   - *Architecture 01*: Heavy metallic beveled Gold Coin with dual gyroscopic orbital halo rings.
   - *Architecture 02*: Refractive 3D Glass Icosahedron Crystal with physical transmission dispersion & embedded core.
   - *Architecture 03*: Topological (2,5) Torus Knot in continuous fluid motion with outer wireframe lattice.
   - *Architecture 04*: 4 Planetary Currency Satellites ($ USD, ₹ INR, £ GBP, € EUR) in gravitational multi-speed orbit.
   - *Architecture 05*: Double-Helix 3D Geometric Capital DNA Growth Ladder compounding upwards.
   - *Architecture 06*: Liquid Mercury Droplet with harmonic undulating vertex ripples.
   - *Architecture 07*: Endless 3D Mobius Continuum twisting in liquid pastel gold space.
   - *Architecture 08*: Multi-Faceted Glass Tetrahedron refracting light rays with dynamic glare.
   - *Architecture 09*: Triple-Axis Mechanical Quantum Gyroscope in calming pastel sage and platinum.
   - *Architecture 10*: Breathing 3D Dodecahedron Opal with multi-light specular dispersion.
2. **Typography Diversity (Never Repeat Type Stacks)**:
   - *Syne & Plus Jakarta Sans* (Sovereign luxury)
   - *Clash Display & Satoshi* (Vercel precision / 21st.dev)
   - *Cabinet Grotesk & Inter* (Raycast cybernetic)
   - *DM Serif Display & Plus Jakarta Sans* (Apple enterprise macro)
   - *Playfair Display & Outfit* (Framer motion luxury)
   - *Fraunces & Manrope* (Warm linear editorial)
   - *Bebas Neue & Work Sans* (High-velocity continuum)
   - *General Sans & Satoshi* (Stripe Press architectural)
   - *Space Grotesk & Inter* (Anthropic technical bastion)
3. **Harmonious Pastel Palettes**: Avoid harsh dark neon gradients. Use light, high-contrast, elegant pastel canvases:
   - Pastel Cream (`#FAF8F5`, `#0B0F1D`, `#06B6D4`)
   - Pastel Lavender (`#F5F3FF`, `#1E1B4B`, `#8B5CF6`)
   - Pastel Mint (`#F0FDF4`, `#064E3B`, `#10B981`)
   - Pastel Peach (`#FFF7ED`, `#7C2D12`, `#F97316`)
   - Pastel Coral (`#FFF1F2`, `#881337`, `#E11D48`)
   - Pastel Sky (`#F0F9FF`, `#0C4A6E`, `#0284C7`)
   - Pastel Butter (`#FEFCE8`, `#713F12`, `#CA8A04`)
   - Pastel Lilac (`#FAF5FF`, `#581C87`, `#9333EA`)
   - Pastel Sage (`#F6FBF7`, `#14532D`, `#16A34A`)
   - Pastel Opal (`#F8FAFC`, `#0F172A`, `#0EA5E9`)
4. **Bespoke Analytical Engines per Build**:
   - Compounding Math Simulator ($A=P(1+r)^t$)
   - 10,000-Path Monte Carlo Probabilistic Fan Chart
   - Cross-Asset Factor Correlation Matrix Heatmap
   - Structural USD/INR FX Spread & G-Sec Arbitrage Telemetry
   - 15-Year Life Cycle Milestone Velocity Roadmap
   - Quarterly Annuity Cashflow Cascade Engine
   - Fama-French 5-Factor Alpha Decomposer
   - Dynamic 70:15:15 Portfolio Rebalancer
   - Section 14 Court Attachment Legal Immunity Shield
   - 200-Module Master Quantitative Codex Table
5. **Clean Prototype Isolation**: Always keep production code (`index.html`) separate from experimental prototype labs (`/prototypes/proto1.html` to `proto10.html` with an overarching `index.html` hub).
