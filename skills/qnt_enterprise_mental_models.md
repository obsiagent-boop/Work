# qnt. Enterprise Mental Models & Execution Framework (The 12-Book Cognitive Architecture)

This document establishes the institutional mental models, decision matrices, and execution workflows synthesized from the world's 12 most practical texts in quantitative finance, value investing, systems thinking, and business monetization.

---

## The 12 Foundational Mental Models for qnt. Enterprise

### 1. The Antifragile Barbell Allocation (Nassim Nicholas Taleb — *Antifragile*)
* **The Principle:** Avoid the vulnerable middle. Allocate 85–90% in sovereign, zero-default bedrock (PPF, SSY, SCSS, 10Y Gilts) with EEE tax exemption, and 10–15% in hyper-convex multipliers (Small-Cap Alpha, US Tech, Out-of-the-Money LEAPS Options).
* **qnt. Implementation:** Encoded into Tier 1 and Tier 4 of the 4-Tier Asset Allocation Architecture.

### 2. Second-Level Thinking & Pendulum Cycles (Howard Marks — *The Most Important Thing*)
* **The Principle:** First-level asks "Is this a good company?" Second-level asks "Does the current price reflect overly optimistic expectations, or has market panic created an asymmetric entry point?"
* **qnt. Implementation:** Dual scenario analysis embedded in every module (`🚀 WEALTH UPSIDE` vs `📉 INFLATION DRAG & RECESSION RISK`).

### 3. Inversion & Cognitive Failure Checklist (Charlie Munger — *Poor Charlie's Almanack*)
* **The Principle:** Invert, always invert. Figure out how an investment or financial plan can be destroyed (inflation, high TER fees, illiquidity, tax drag, single-stock bankruptcy), and systematically eliminate those vulnerabilities.
* **qnt. Implementation:** Critical Risks, Lock-in Periods, and Failure Modes box in all 200 modules.

### 4. Intrinsic DCF & Reverse DCF Expectations (Aswath Damodaran — *Investment Valuation*)
* **The Principle:** Intrinsic value equals the discounted sum of future free cash flows. Reverse DCF calculates the implied growth rate priced into the current market price. True wealth is created only when $\text{ROIC} > \text{WACC}$.
* **qnt. Implementation:** Valuation metrics, Return on Capital benchmarks, and low-cost Direct Plan recommendations.

### 5. Triple Barrier Method & Deflated Sharpe (Marcos López de Prado — *Advances in Financial ML*)
* **The Principle:** Replace fixed-time horizons with dynamic barriers (Upper Profit Target, Lower Stop Loss, Maximum Holding Period) to eliminate overfitting.
* **qnt. Implementation:** Telemetry metrics (Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Max Drawdown, Settlement Horizon) embedded in every module.

### 6. Statistical Arbitrage & Cointegration Spreads (Ernie Chan — *Quantitative Trading*)
* **The Principle:** Trade mean-reverting stationary spreads between correlated asset classes (Cash vs Futures on NSE, Spot Gold vs Secondary SGB discounts).
* **qnt. Implementation:** Advanced cashflow methodologies (Arbitrage Cash Moats, SGB Secondary Market Harvesting).

### 7. Theta Decay Physics & Covered Options (John C. Hull — *Options, Futures & Derivatives*)
* **The Principle:** Time value of options decays non-linearly ($\Theta$). Selling 1-month 5%–7% OTM Covered Calls converts market sideways consolidation into immediate broker wallet cashflow without surrendering underlying shares.
* **qnt. Implementation:** Systematic Covered Call overlays generating 8%–14% annualized non-directional yield.

### 8. The Kelly Criterion for Position Sizing (Edward O. Thorp — *A Man for All Markets*)
* **The Principle:** Size investments proportionally to mathematical edge ($f^* = \frac{bp - q}{b}$) to maximize geometric capital compounding while guaranteeing zero ruin probability.
* **qnt. Implementation:** Exact allocation sizing tiers (₹1,000 Starter, ₹10,000 Core SIP, ₹1,00,000 Lump-Sum).

### 9. Stocks, Flows & Feedback Leverage Points (Donella Meadows — *Thinking in Systems*)
* **The Principle:** Systems are governed by stock accumulations, inflow rates, outflow drains, and feedback loops. Adjusting high-leverage points yields exponential systemic change with minimal force.
* **qnt. Implementation:** 3-Bucket Retirement Cashflow Waterfall and SWP automated paycheck routing.

### 10. The All-Weather 4-Quadrant Machine (Ray Dalio — *Principles*)
* **The Principle:** Balance asset holdings across the 4 macroeconomic regimes: Growth Rising, Growth Falling, Inflation Rising, Inflation Falling.
* **qnt. Implementation:** Asset Correlation Matrix ensuring negative/uncorrelated behavior between Indian Equities (+1.0), US Tech (+0.38), Sovereign Debt (-0.12), and Gold (-0.08).

### 11. The Grand Slam Value Equation (Alex Hormozi — *$100M Offers*)
* **The Principle:** $\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Dream Outcome} \times \text{Perceived Likelihood}}{\text{Time Delay} \times \text{Effort \& Sacrifice}}$.
* **qnt. Implementation:** The 5-Step UI Application Pipeline and Real-Time Profit Realization cards that eliminate investor friction and clarify exact payout mechanisms.

### 12. Theory of Constraints Bottleneck Removal (Eliyahu Goldratt — *The Goal*)
* **The Principle:** System throughput is determined solely by the primary bottleneck. In personal finance, the primary bottlenecks are high fund manager fees (TER), dividend tax drag, and emotional market timing.
* **qnt. Implementation:** Systematic elimination of fees via Direct-Growth plans (TER < 0.20%) and tax elimination via SWP Section 112A exemptions.
