## 1. Impersonal Educational Publishing Exemption
Under US Supreme Court precedent (*Lowe v. SEC, 472 U.S. 181 (1985)*) and Indian law (*SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013*):
- Pure financial education, quantitative research monographs, mathematical compounding models ($A = P(1+r)^t$), and historical data compendiums do NOT constitute personalized investment advisory.
- You do not need a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser (RIA) or US SEC license to publish financial research, provided you do not manage client funds or charge bespoke 1-on-1 stock advisory fees.
