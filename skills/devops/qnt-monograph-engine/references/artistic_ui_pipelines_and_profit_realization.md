## 2. The Dedicated Real-Time Profit Realization Box
Every module must specify the exact cadence and mechanics of receiving returns:

```html
<div class="payout-box">
  <strong>💰 REAL-TIME PROFIT REALIZATION &amp; PAYOUT SCHEDULE:</strong><br/>
  {payout_info}
</div>
```

### Specific Asset-Class Payout Cadences:
- **POMIS:** Guaranteed monthly cash interest auto-credited to POSA savings on 1st of every month. 100% liquid for monthly household expenses.
- **SCSS:** Quarterly sovereign cashflow (8.2% p.a.) credited on 1st of Apr/Jul/Oct/Jan via ECS NEFT direct to bank account.
- **PPF:** Annual tax-free compounding interest credited on March 31. Partial tax-free cash withdrawals unlocked from Year 7 onwards.
- **SSY:** 8.2% annual tax-free compounding. 50% corpus unlocked at age 18 for university education, 100% full tax-free maturity payout at 21 years.
- **REITs / InvITs:** Quarterly rental distributions (Interest + Dividend + SPV Capital Repayment) deposited directly into bank account on SEBI NDCF schedules.
- **G-Secs / Corporate Bonds:** Semi-annual or monthly fixed interest coupons credited directly via RBI Clearing / RTGS into linked bank account.
- **SGB Gold:** 2.50% annual cash interest paid semi-annually + 100% tax-free capital gains realized directly in bank upon 8-year RBI redemption.
- **Equities & Mutual Funds:** Automated monthly Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP) or annual rebalancing harvest utilizing the ₹1.25 Lakh tax-free LTCG exemption under Section 112A.
