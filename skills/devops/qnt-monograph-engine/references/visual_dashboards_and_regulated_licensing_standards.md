# Visual Analytical Dashboards, Regulated Industry Licensing & 100+ Page Scale Standards

## 1. Visual Analytical Dashboard Widgets in PDF (WeasyPrint Compatible)
Because WeasyPrint converts HTML/CSS to PDF without executing live client-side JavaScript canvas scripts, analytical dashboards must be structured natively using CSS Flexbox/Grid and high-density SVG vectors:
- **4-Metric Visual KPI Grid:** Every business entry must lead with a 4-box analytical KPI widget (Avg. Monthly Revenue, Gross Margin %, CAC Payback Days, Marketing ROAS / LTV:CAC).
- **Styling Architecture:** Crisp white cards (`#FFFFFF`) with 1px pitch-black borders (`#000000`), micro-labels (`6.5pt font-weight: 800`), and bold values (`8pt font-weight: 900`).
- **Telemetry Narrative:** Directly follow the KPI widget with an explicit Quantitative Analytics & Performance Telemetry Model paragraph detailing unit economics and cashflow velocity.

## 2. Regulated Industry Licensing Blueprints (e.g. Canadian Cannabis Retail)
When researching regulated storefronts, dispensaries, or healthcare operations:
- **Exact Licensing Tiers:** Include the exact provincial application fees, operator licenses (e.g. Ontario AGCO Cannabis Retail Operator Licence - CROL $6,000 CAD), and physical retail store authorizations (RSA $4,000 CAD).
- **Zoning & Security Mandates:** Detail mandatory buffer zones (e.g. 150m from schools), public notice placards, 24/7 CCTV recording coverage, locked vault requirements, and employee certifications (e.g. CannSell Ontario $65 CAD).
- **Wholesale Monopolies & Supply Chains:** Document the government wholesale portal (e.g. Ontario Cannabis Store - OCS B2B) and standard wholesale gross margins (28%–42%).

## 3. Academic & Immigration Pathway Monetization (e.g. U of T Masters & PGWP)
When analyzing student/graduate monetization:
- **Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) Leverage:** Detail exact streams that do not require a Canadian job offer (e.g. OINP Masters Graduate Stream).
- **Work Permit Business Ownership:** Detail the legal right for Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) and Spousal Open Work Permit holders to incorporate federal corporations under Bill C-25 with zero director residency requirements.
- **B2B Incorporation Strategy:** Structure high-margin consulting agencies (AI engineering for Bay Street, academic admissions, quantitative research) allowing founders to retain earnings at the 12.2% small business tax rate.
