# Executive Research Methodology & Capital-Sorted Hierarchy Standards

## Core Principles
1. **Initial Executive Research Pages (Pages 1–3):**
   - **Page 1:** Institutional Title, Regulatory Frameworks, and Legal Disclaimers.
   - **Page 2:** **Comprehensive Executive Methodology & Source Attribution Table** detailing every official authority investigated, data collection methods, and direct endpoints with zero data loss.
   - **Page 3:** **Master Capital Slab Hierarchy Index** outlining the strict low-to-high progression tiers ($0–$100 CAD to $150,000+ CAD).

2. **Strict Ascending Capital Hierarchy ($0–$100 CAD to $150,000+ CAD):**
   - Business models MUST NOT be listed in random order. They must be sorted strictly from lowest to highest startup capital requirements:
     - Tier 01: Zero-Cost & Micro-Capital Foundations ($0 – $100 CAD)
     - Tier 02: Micro-Capital Digital Agencies ($100 – $250 CAD)
     - Tier 03: High-Margin Technical & RevOps Consulting ($150 – $500 CAD)
     - Tier 04: E-Commerce, Retail Arbitrage & Light Mobile Services ($500 – $1,000 CAD)
     - Tier 05: Essential Mobile Trades & Residential Restoration ($1,000 – $2,000 CAD)
     - Tier 06: Commercial Janitorial, Cold-Climate & Food Services ($2,000 – $3,500 CAD)
     - Tier 07: Fleet Logistics, Hot-Shot Transport & Asset Rental ($3,500 – $6,000 CAD)
     - Tier 08: Healthcare Staffing, Contractor Fleets & Specialized Assets ($6,000 – $10,000 CAD)
     - Tier 09: Regulated Cannabis Retail Storefronts & Infrastructure ($10,000 – $150,000+ CAD)

3. **Resolved Red-Dot 5-Point Component Architecture:**
   - **Dual-Chart Visual Analytics Dashboard Panel:** 4 distinct KPI metric cards (*Monthly Run-Rate*, *Gross Margin %*, *Net EBITDA*, *CAC Payback*) + vector SVG 12-Month Revenue Growth curves + horizontal Expense Distribution stacked bar charts.
   - **Capital Telemetry Sub-Row in Economic Tables:** Explicit breakdown of startup fees, reserve capital, and Bill C-25 director residency rules.
   - **In-Depth Business Model Architecture:** Dense, analytical paragraphs detailing unit economics, customer acquisition, and gross margins.
   - **Granular Step-by-Step Implementation Sequences:** Municipal licensing, WSIB/WorkSafeBC registrations, CGL insurance, and banking rails.
   - **Continuous Telemetry & Verified Government Endpoints.**
