# Fresh Domain Isolation & Zero-Cross-Pollination Research Standards

## Core Trigger & Directive
When the user requests fresh, intensive research into a specific target domain (e.g., Canadian business models, immigration legal frameworks, commercial monetization slabs, real estate, physical trades):
1. **Strict Context Isolation:** Completely purge mental models, software toolsets (e.g., ServiceNow, Power BI, coding IDEs), and methodologies from prior turns unless explicitly requested for the current task.
2. **Pure Domain Authenticity:** The research must originate 100% from authentic, real-world data of the target topic (e.g., Canada Business Corporations Act, CRA GST/HST rules, provincial corporate registries, municipal licensing, Transport Canada regulations).
3. **100+ Page Continuous Zero-Void Standard:**
   - To achieve 50–100+ pages of authentic dense research without voids, scale the catalog to 120–240+ granular, deeply analyzed operational opportunities.
   - Flow all data continuously from top to bottom margin using compact line-height (`1.32–1.34`), Inter Variable font, and Luxury Cream (`#FAF8F5`) canvas with pitch-black (`#000000`) typography.
   - Bounded elements (summary tables, callouts) must use `break-inside: avoid;` (under 100px) so headers never disconnect from tables.
4. **End Master Bibliography Requirement:** Embed all verified government endpoints, legal registries, official portals, and tool repositories in a dedicated, centralized Master Bibliography (e.g., Sector 12/16) at the end of the compendium.
