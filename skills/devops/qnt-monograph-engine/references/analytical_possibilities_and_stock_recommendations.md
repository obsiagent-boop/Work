# Analytical Possibilities, Wealth Multipliers, Depreciation & Stock Recommendations

This reference defines the enterprise standards for embedding analytical dimensions and stock/ETF/bond recommendations into every instrument within `qnt.` research monographs.

## The 3 Analytical Possibility Sections

Every fund profile must contain these three structured blocks to provide balanced, comprehensive analysis to the public:

### 1. `🚀 ANALYTICAL POSSIBILITIES: WEALTH CREATION & MULTIPLIER UPSIDE`
- Explain the specific compounding mechanics of the asset (e.g. geometric reinvestment, tax-free Triple-E accrual, earnings yield growth, monopoly pricing power).
- Quantify how the instrument outpaces nominal inflation (CPI >6.5%) and builds a generational financial moat.
- Motivate disciplined long-term accumulation without making illegal guarantees.

### 2. `📉 ANALYTICAL POSSIBILITIES: CAPITAL DEPRECIATION & INFLATION DRAG`
- Objectively analyze what happens if an investor fails to invest or holds cash (real purchasing power destruction).
- Highlight structural risks: asset illiquidity, interest rate cycle shifts, sequence of returns risk (SRR), and post-tax drag.
- Provide a clear warning on potential capital loss during market corrections.

### 3. `💡 HIGH-CONVICTION DIRECT ASSET / STOCK RECOMMENDATIONS`
- Name the exact direct-growth mutual fund folios, sovereign primary issuance tranches, or exchange tickers.
- Standard Tickers:
  - **US Equities:** `VOO` (S&P 500), `QQQ` (Nasdaq 100), `SMH` (Semiconductors).
  - **Domestic Factor ETFs:** `MOMENTUM` (Nifty 200 Momentum 30), `NV20` (Nifty 50 Value 20).
  - **Commodities:** `GOLDBEES` (Gold ETF), `SILVERBEES` (Silver ETF), `SGB` secondary tranches.
  - **Real Estate & Infra:** `EMBASSY` (Embassy REIT), `MINDSPACE` (Mindspace REIT), `PGINVIT` (PowerGrid InvIT).
- Enforce Total Expense Ratio (TER) discipline (<0.30% for index funds/ETFs).
