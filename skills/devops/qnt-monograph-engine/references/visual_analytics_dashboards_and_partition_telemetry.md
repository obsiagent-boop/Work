# Visual Analytics Dashboards, Partition Table Telemetry & Zero-Void Continuous Flow Standards

## Core Layout Directives for Visual Dashboards & Business Monograph Modules

### 1. Dual-Chart Visual Analytics Dashboard Panels
Every commercial model and business opportunity module MUST lead with an isolated, high-contrast visual analytics panel (`.visual-dashboard-panel`) structured as follows:
- **Header Bar:** Distinct title (e.g. `📊 EXECUTIVE FINANCIAL TELEMETRY & MONETIZATION DASHBOARD`) + technology badge (`Power BI / Tableau Pipeline`).
- **4-Metric KPI Grid:**
  - `Monthly Run-Rate` (Formatted in target currency, e.g. `$125,000 CAD`)
  - `Gross Margin %` (Percentage benchmark, e.g. `34.5%` or `78.2%`)
  - `Net EBITDA` (Monthly operating cashflow after direct costs)
  - `CAC Payback` (Payback velocity in days or months, e.g. `14 Days`)
- **Dual Vector SVG Analytical Charts (Side-by-Side Grid):**
  1. *Left Chart:* 12-Month Revenue Growth Vector Line Curve with linear gradient area fill (`<svg>` with `<defs>`, `<linearGradient>`, `<polygon>`, and `<polyline>`).
  2. *Right Chart:* Expense Distribution Breakdown Horizontal Stacked Bar Chart (`<rect>` bars displaying COGS %, Labor %, and Net Profit Margin %).

### 2. Economic Summary Tables & Partition Telemetry Rows
Every summary table MUST include an explicit, detailed telemetry sub-row spanning all columns (`colspan="4"`):
- Detail the exact capital breakdown: upfront filing fees ($200–$300 CAD), initial equipment leasing, commercial insurance binders, and mandatory reserve liquidity.
- Detail the exact legal/visa telemetry: explain how Open Work Permits, PGWP holders, and Permanent Residents maintain legal capacity to incorporate as 100% sole directors under Bill C-25 without Canadian residency restrictions in Ontario, BC, and Alberta.

### 3. Granular Step-by-Step Implementation & Regulatory Onboarding
Implementation roadmaps must never be brief 1-line bullet points. They must detail:
- Step 1: Corporate entity registration and NUANS name clearance.
- Step 2: CRA Business Number (BN), Corporate Tax (RC), and GST/HST (RT) account provisioning.
- Step 3: Provincial safety board compliance (WSIB in Ontario, WorkSafeBC in BC) and $2M–$5M Commercial General Liability (CGL) insurance.
- Step 4: Specific industry licensing (e.g. AGCO CROL/RSA and CannSell for cannabis; CVOR/NSC for freight; municipal food handler permits for ghost kitchens).
- Step 5: Commercial bank account setup (Interac e-Transfer, EFT, Pre-Authorized Debit) and point-of-sale deployment.

### 4. 100% Zero-Void Continuous Flow
- Never allow table telemetry or quantitative models to fracture across awkward page breaks.
- Use `break-after: avoid;` on title bars and section labels.
- Allow dense text paragraphs, tables, and dashboard cards to flow naturally and continuously from the top header line to the bottom footer margin.
