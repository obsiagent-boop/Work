# Authoritative Publication Voice & Section Architecture Standard

## Purpose
Establishes the editorial voice, tone, and section hierarchy for all `qnt.` quantitative monographs, compendiums, and executive research dossiers. Ensures all deliverables read as world-class institutional publications with zero prompt artifacts, robotic conversational filler, or self-referential commentary.

---

## 1. Core Voice Principles
- **Authoritative & Objective:** Deliver deep financial research, macroeconomic analysis, and mathematical proofs from the perspective of an institutional quantitative research firm.
- **Zero Prompt Meta-Commentary:** Never include phrases like:
  - *"as per your commands"*
  - *"100% bespoke"*
  - *"as requested in prompt"*
  - *"I have updated this section"*
- **Natural Institutional Phrasing:** Frame analysis around macroeconomic mechanisms, regulatory statutes, historical empirical data, and quantitative compounding dynamics.

---

## 2. Standard 11-Section Module Hierarchy

