# 100% Bespoke Domain Analytics & Elimination of Repetitive Section Boilerplate

## Pitfall Identified
When generating multi-module reports, models often fall into the trap of using string templates for sectional blocks like `🚀 ANALYTICAL POSSIBILITIES: WEALTH CREATION & MULTIPLIER UPSIDE`, `📉 ANALYTICAL POSSIBILITIES: CAPITAL DEPRECIATION & INFLATION DRAG`, and `💡 HIGH-CONVICTION DIRECT INSTRUMENT / STOCK RECOMMENDATIONS`. 
This results in repeating the exact same sentence (e.g., *"Deploying capital systematically into [Name] enables compounding acceleration that outpaces nominal consumer price inflation..."*) across all 200 modules, which breaks reader immersion and ruins enterprise credibility.

## Mandatory Architectural Rule
Every single instrument MUST receive genuinely bespoke text for all 3 analytical sections:

1. **`🚀 WEALTH CREATION & MULTIPLIER UPSIDE`:**
   - Must name the specific compounding mechanism, tax exemption section (e.g. Section 10(11A) for SSY, Section 10(11) for PPF, Section 112A for equities), and realistic target corpus (e.g. ₹70 Lakhs for girl child education in SSY).
   - Must explain how the underlying asset generates returns (rental yields from Fortune 500 tech tenants for REITs, power tariffs for InvITs, corporate earnings growth for Nifty 50).

2. **`📉 CAPITAL DEPRECIATION & INFLATION DRAG`:**
   - Must articulate the exact downside risk unique to that asset (e.g., 21-year lock-in liquidity traps for SSY, marginal tax bracket drag up to 39% for SCSS/POMIS, interest rate hike sensitivity for G-Secs/REITs, 35%–50% peak-to-trough drawdowns for Small Caps).
   - Must quantify the inflation/opportunity cost drag when compared to alternate asset classes.

3. **`💡 HIGH-CONVICTION DIRECT ASSET / STOCK RECOMMENDATIONS`:**
   - Must provide exact executable instructions (e.g., *"Deposit between April 1 and April 5 in PPF to earn full-year interest"*, *"Allocate via UTI Nifty 50 Index Direct-Growth Plan with TER < 0.20%"*, *"Buy Embassy REIT (NSE: EMBASSY) on Demat"*).
