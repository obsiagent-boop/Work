# Global Business & Monetization Research Standards (Canada & Global Markets)

## 1. Overview
When tasked with conducting market research for incorporating, funding, scaling, and monetizing businesses in specific jurisdictions (e.g. Canada, US, EU, Middle East, India), enforce this institutional framework.

## 2. Core Pillars of Research
1. **Legal & Incorporation Structures:**
   - Detailed filing costs (Federal vs. Provincial/State).
   - Director residency rules (e.g. Bill C-25 in Canada repealing federal residency rules).
   - Corporate tax rates, small business deductions (e.g. Canadian CCPC 9% federal rate), and registration fees.
2. **Taxation & Thresholds:**
   - Sales tax rules (GST/HST $30,000 small supplier threshold in Canada, QST, PST).
   - Input Tax Credits (ITCs) and payroll accounts.
3. **Visa & Immigration Pathways for Business Owners:**
   - Work permit holder rights, Open Work Permits, PGWP self-employment.
   - Significant benefit owner-operator programs (e.g. C11 Work Permit in Canada, Intra-Company Transfers - ICT).
4. **Capital Tier Slabs ($100 to $100,000+):**
   - Provide concrete business opportunities across distinct capital tiers (Zero-Cost, $100–$500, $500–$2,500, $2,500–$10,000, $10,000+).
   - Include realistic monthly revenue projections and time-to-first-dollar.
5. **Continuous Zero-Void Layout Standard:**
   - Use continuous prose with structured summary tables immediately below.
   - Prohibit forced page breaks and empty card padding.
   - Embed a comprehensive, centralized Master Bibliography at the end of the compendium indexing all government, regulatory, and corporate endpoints.
