# In-Situ Contextual Financial Jargon Decoders & Educational Architecture

## Core Requirement
Every single financial instrument and volume across qnt. publications MUST feature an in-situ contextual jargon decoder. Never assume the reader knows financial terms. Define every technical term used in that exact module or section directly on that page.

## Key Terminology Lexicon & Plain-Language Explanations

### 1. Sovereign & Fixed Income Terms
- **"EEE Status" (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt):** A triple tax exemption where (1) initial deposits reduce taxable income under Section 80C, (2) annual compounding interest is 100% tax-free, and (3) final maturity withdrawals are completely tax-free.
- **"Court Attachment Immunity":** Under statutory provisions (e.g. Section 14 of the PPF Act), funds cannot be attached, seized, or frozen by any court or creditor during lawsuits or bankruptcy.
- **"Section 10(11A)":** The clause in the Indian Income Tax Act ensuring that Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY) interest and maturity proceeds remain 100% tax-exempt.
- **"Section 80TTB":** Allows senior citizens (60+) to claim up to ₹50,000 in annual interest income from banks and post offices completely tax-free.
- **"Yield-to-Maturity (YTM)":** The total annualized rate of return anticipated on a bond if held until its maturity date.

### 2. Equity & Factor Market Terms
- **"Nifty 50 Index":** A basket of India's 50 largest, most profitable enterprises (Reliance, TCS, HDFC Bank) representing the core engine of the Indian economy.
- **"Total Expense Ratio (TER)":** The annual fee charged by mutual funds (e.g., 0.05% to 0.50%) to manage assets. Lower TER compounds significantly more wealth over time.
- **"LTCG (Long-Term Capital Gains Tax)":** Gains from equity units held >12 months are taxed at a flat 12.5% only on profits exceeding ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year (Section 112A).
- **"STCG (Short-Term Capital Gains Tax)":** Gains from equity units sold within 12 months are taxed at a flat 20% under Section 111A.
- **"Alpha":** The excess return generated by an active fund or factor strategy over the baseline market benchmark index.
- **"Beta":** Market sensitivity multiplier. 1.0 moves in lockstep with the market; >1.0 amplifies swings (e.g., Small Caps @ 1.15); <1.0 dampens volatility (e.g., Gilts @ 0.05).

### 3. Quantitative Risk & Downside Metrics
- **"Sharpe Ratio":** Ratio of excess return over risk-free rate divided by standard deviation (reward per unit of total risk; >1.2 is institutional grade).
- **"Sortino Ratio":** Like Sharpe, but only penalizes harmful downside volatility. >2.0 indicates robust crash protection.
- **"Max Historical Drawdown":** The largest peak-to-trough decline experienced in history, defining the worst-case drawdown bound.

### 4. Real Estate, Debt & Advanced Cashflow Terms
- **"REIT (Real Estate Investment Trust)":** Fractional ownership in Grade-A commercial office parks leased to multinational tenants.
- **"NDCF (Net Distributable Cash Flow)":** SEBI statutory rule mandating that REITs distribute at least 90% of net cash income quarterly to unitholders.
- **"Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) Coupon":** A 2.50% annual cash interest paid semi-annually by RBI in addition to tracking 100% of gold price appreciation.
- **"RBI LRS (Liberalized Remittance Scheme)":** Central bank framework permitting Indian residents to legally remit up to $250,000 USD overseas annually for investments.
- **"Systematic Withdrawal Plan (SWP)":** Automated monthly share redemptions structured primarily as return of capital, minimizing taxes.
- **"Loan Against Securities (LAS)":** Pledging mutual funds/SGBs to secure a low-cost revolving bank overdraft without triggering capital gains taxes.
