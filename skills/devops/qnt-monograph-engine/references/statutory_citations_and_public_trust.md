## Citation Format By Asset Category

### 1. Sovereign Government & Post Office Schemes
- **PPF:** Public Provident Fund Scheme 2019, Section 80C & Section 10(11) of Indian Income Tax Act 1961.
- **SSY:** Sukanya Samriddhi Account Scheme 2019 & Ministry of Women & Child Development Gazette G.S.R. 914(E).
- **SCSS:** Senior Citizens Savings Scheme Rules 2019 & Finance Act Amendment (Gazette Notification G.S.R. 916(E)).
- **POMIS:** National Savings (Monthly Income Account) Scheme 2019 & G.S.R. 917(E) Ministry of Finance Gazette.
- **NSC:** National Savings Certificates (VIII Issue) Scheme 2019 & G.S.R. 919(E) Ministry of Finance Gazette.
- **KVP:** Kisan Vikas Patra Scheme 2019 & Ministry of Finance Gazette Notification G.S.R. 920(E).
- **General Rules:** Government Savings Promotion General Rules 2018 (G.S.R. 1236(E)).

### 2. Direct Debt & RBI Sovereign Instruments
- **RBI Retail Direct:** RBI Retail Direct Scheme Notification (RBI/2021-22/123) & Section 45W of Reserve Bank of India Act 1934.
- **T-Bills & G-Secs:** Public Debt Act 1944, Government Securities Act 2006 & FIMMDA Benchmark Valuation Standards.
- **State Development Loans (SDLs):** Article 293(3) of Constitution of India & RBI State Finance Reports.
- **SGB:** Government of India Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme Gazette Notifications & Section 47(viic) Income Tax Act.

### 3. Mutual Funds, Smart Beta & Index ETFs
- **Equity Categorization:** SEBI Mutual Fund Categorization Circular (SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2017/114).
- **Multi-Cap Mandate:** SEBI Circular on Asset Allocation of Multi Cap Funds (SEBI/HO/IMD/DF3/CIR/P/2020/172).
- **Index Methodologies:** NSE Indices Nifty 50 / Next 50 / Midcap 150 / Smallcap 250 Index Methodologies & S&P Dow Jones SPIVA India Scorecard.
- **ELSS:** Section 80C of Income Tax Act 1961 & Equity Linked Savings Scheme Guidelines 2005.

### 4. Commercial REITs & InvITs
- **REITs:** SEBI (Real Estate Investment Trusts) Regulations 2014 & Clause 18 (90% NDCF Distribution Policy).
- **InvITs:** SEBI (Infrastructure Investment Trusts) Regulations 2014 & CERC Tariff Regulations.
- **SM REITs:** SEBI (Real Estate Investment Trusts) (Amendment) Regulations 2024.
- **Taxation:** Section 10(23FD), Section 115UA & Section 194LBA of Indian Income Tax Act 1961.

### 5. Global Cross-Border Assets (RBI LRS)
- **LRS Remittance:** RBI Master Direction on Liberalised Remittance Scheme (FED Master Direction No. 7/2015-16).
- **TCS 20%:** Section 206C(1G) of Indian Income Tax Act 1961 as amended by Finance Act 2023.
- **US Securities:** US SEC Form N-1A / Form 10-K Registrations & US Internal Revenue Service (IRS) Section 1441 W-8BEN Treaty.
- **Compliance:** Black Money (Undisclosed Foreign Income and Assets) and Imposition of Tax Act 2015 (Schedule FA).
