# Real-Time AI Gigs, Operations & Customer Support Platform Playbook

## Top 6 Real-Time Payout AI Platforms (Non-Developer Friendly)

### 1. Outlier AI (Scale AI)
- **Role Scope:** AI Model Trainer, RLHF Prompt Evaluator, Multi-turn reasoning assessment.
- **Pay Range:** $18 to $40 USD / hour.
- **Payout Rails:** Weekly / Instant via PayPal or AirTM.
- **Application Strategy:** Complete standard writing and logic onboarding tests. Emphasize factual accuracy and clear rationale formulation.

### 2. Alignerr (Powered by Labelbox)
- **Role Scope:** AI Output Quality Analyst, Safety, Red-Teaming, Tone Evaluation.
- **Pay Range:** $20 to $60 USD / hour.
- **Payout Rails:** Weekly via Deel or Direct Bank Transfer.
- **Application Strategy:** Demonstrate understanding of LLM hallucination modes and linguistic nuances.

### 3. DataAnnotation.tech
- **Role Scope:** Chatbot persona evaluation, creative content rating, factual verification.
- **Pay Range:** $20 to $42+ USD / hour.
- **Payout Rails:** On-demand instant cashout via PayPal (after 7-day initial hold).
- **Application Strategy:** High attention to detail on preliminary qualification tests.

### 4. Invisible Technologies
- **Role Scope:** AI Operations Specialist, Process Operator, Human-in-the-Loop data workflows.
- **Pay Range:** $15 to $35 USD / hour.
- **Payout Rails:** Bi-weekly direct bank wire.
- **Application Strategy:** Highlight spreadsheet literacy, Airtable/Notion database organization, and reliable turn-around speed.

### 5. ModSquad
- **Role Scope:** Remote Customer Support Specialist, AI Ticket Moderator, Omnichannel CX.
- **Pay Range:** $15 to $28 USD / hour.
- **Payout Rails:** Bi-weekly / Monthly via direct deposit.
- **Application Strategy:** Emphasize Zendesk, Intercom, Freshdesk experience and customer de-escalation skills.

### 6. OneForma (Centific)
- **Role Scope:** AI Prompt Annotation, Search Engine Rating, Audio Transcription.
- **Pay Range:** $12 to $25 USD / hour.
- **Payout Rails:** Monthly via Payoneer and PayPal.
- **Application Strategy:** Ideal for entry-level side gigs and flexible part-time task queues.
