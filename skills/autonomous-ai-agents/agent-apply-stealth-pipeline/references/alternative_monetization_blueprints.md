## Detailed Execution Playbooks

### 1. The "Cold-Audit Redesign" Strategy ($500 – $1,500 / deal)
- **Step 1:** Search Google Maps for local businesses (clinics, dentists, gyms, law firms) or US startups with slow/non-responsive websites.
- **Step 2:** Ask Hermes: *"Build a luxury dark-mode landing page for [Business Name]."* (Hermes outputs clean HTML5/Tailwind CSS in 60 seconds).
- **Step 3:** Host for free on GitHub Pages / Vercel, and email/DM the owner:
  > *"Hey [Name], I noticed your site was losing mobile traffic, so I redesigned your homepage with sub-50ms load speed. Here is the live demo link. If you like it, I can connect it to your domain for $500."*
- **Why it works:** Bypasses cold-pitch skepticism because the finished product is demonstrated upfront. Conversion rates exceed 25%.

### 2. Selling Digital Products on LemonSqueezy & Gumroad ($1,000+/mo Passive)
- **Store Setup:** Create free accounts on [LemonSqueezy.com](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/) and [Gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com/).
- **High-Demand Digital Packs to Generate:**
  1. *2026 Remote Job Hunting OS (Notion Template)* — Built in minutes via Notion API/Markdown.
  2. *100 High-Converting Customer Support Prompt Macros* — Pre-crafted Zendesk/Intercom responses.
  3. *SaaS Landing Page HTML5 Template Kit* — Responsive dark-mode layouts from `/data/project_agent_apply/services/web_builder/`.
- **Unit Economics:** Selling at $29 with 40 monthly purchases generates **$1,160 USD (~₹1.0 Lakh/mo)** passive income deposited directly into your bank.

### 3. Remote App Testing on uTest & Testlio ($20–$50 / hr + Bounties)
- **Platforms:** Register on [uTest.com](https://www.utest.com/) and [Testlio.com](https://testlio.com/).
- **Hermes Assistance:** Ask Hermes to generate edge-case API tests, network latency simulations, or format technical reproduction steps with bug logs for maximum bounty payouts ($10–$50 per valid bug).
