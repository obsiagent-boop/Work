# Agent Ghost-Delivery & Global Payout Pipelines Playbook

## 1. The Operational Ghost-Delivery Model
1. **Bid & Win Client Gigs:** You accept freelance jobs on Contra (0% platform cut), Upwork, Fiverr, or direct outreach on X/Twitter/LinkedIn for landing pages, web development, financial research compendiums, or AI automation bots.
2. **Hermes Autonomous Build Engine:** You forward client requirements to Hermes. Hermes instantly generates production-grade responsive HTML5/Tailwind code, WeasyPrint research monographs, or Python workflow automations.
3. **Review & Handover:** You review the deliverable and hand it directly to your employer/client under your own brand identity.
4. **Direct Client Payout:** You keep 100% of client fees ($300 to $3,500+ per project) delivered at 10x market velocity.

## 3. The 5 Direct Global-to-India Payment Pipelines
1. **Wise Business / Personal (Recommended):**
   - Client sends USD/EUR via local bank transfer or debit card.
   - Automatically converts to INR and deposits into SBI/HDFC/ICICI bank account via NEFT/IMPS within 24 hours.
   - Fees: ~0.5% (Lowest FX spread in the world).
   - Generates automatic digital **e-FIRA (Foreign Inward Remittance Advice)** for zero tax friction.
2. **Payoneer:**
   - Client pays via ACH wire, credit card, or Upwork/Fiverr freelance balance.
   - Auto-withdraws to Indian bank account in 1–2 business days (Fee: ~1.5%–2.0%).
   - Provides automated downloadable digital FIRC certificates.
3. **Contra Direct Payouts:**
   - Clients pay via Stripe / Credit Card with **0% Contra platform commission**.
   - Payout directly to Indian bank account via Stripe / Payoneer in 24–48 hours.
4. **Stripe via US Wyoming LLC (Project Anya Model):**
   - Direct credit card billing on custom invoices; funds deposited into US Mercury/Relay bank and remitted to India via Wise.
5. **Crypto / USDC (Solana / Ethereum / Polygon):**
   - Instant sub-second client payments to non-custodial wallet; off-ramped to INR via CoinDCX or P2P.

## 4. Indian Tax Optimization & Zero-Audit Exemption
- **Section 44ADA (Presumptive Taxation):** For gross freelance receipts up to ₹75 Lakhs/year, declare only 50% as taxable profit with zero CA audit requirements.
- **0% GST (Export of Services):** File a free Letter of Undertaking (LUT) on the GST portal to classify foreign earnings as zero-rated exports.
